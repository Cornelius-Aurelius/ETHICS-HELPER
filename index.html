◎

Built for your privacy.

Your reflections stay in your browser. No account, analytics tracker or external AI request. Nothing is saved automatically. If you select “Save private draft,” it is stored only in this browser's local storage, where others using this device may access it.

You can clear the saved draft at any time. Avoid typing sensitive personal information.

✳

When the stakes are higher.

For urgent danger, call your local emergency number. In the UK and Ireland, Samaritans offer free emotional support on 116 123. In the US, call or text 988 for crisis support. Elsewhere, find local services through Find A Helpline.

This tool cannot replace timely professional or emergency support.
